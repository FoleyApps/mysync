Never miss a plan you made

PlanFetch reads the messages and email you let it see, spots the plans in them and proposes calendar events. You confirm each one with a tap.

PlanFetch was called mySync until November 20, 2026.

Read on your iPhone

Apple Intelligence reads your messages on the iPhone itself. PlanFetch has no servers, accounts, analytics or ads.

Nothing added without you

Every plan waits for your tap on Add. A plan that moves or gets canceled asks first too.

Your whole schedule

See every calendar on your iPhone in one place, with overlaps called out and free times nearby.

What you need

iOS 26.4 or later, and an iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence, or an iPhone 12 Pro, 13 Pro, 14 or newer, which reads with a model PlanFetch downloads. Reading messages automatically as they arrive needs iOS 27. PlanFetch reads English for now.
