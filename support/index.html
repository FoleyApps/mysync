What you need

An iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence turned on, and iOS 26.4 or later. Reading messages automatically as they arrive needs iOS 27. mySync reads English for now.

Common questions

mySync says it needs Apple Intelligence

Turn it on in iOS Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. mySync continues by itself once it's on. If the model is still downloading, plans wait until it finishes.

A text didn't become a plan

Open the Inbox's Activity view, which lists every message read and what came of it. If the text isn't there, the Read My Texts shortcut may not have run. Check the Messages page in Sources, and if the automation is off, use Open in Shortcuts to turn it on: Edit, the › on its "When" line, then Automation.

A plan has the wrong time or place

Tap the plan and edit any field before you add it. If it was read wrong, Read Again in History or Activity reads it once more from what's kept of the message.

How do I connect an email account?

In Sources, tap Connect on the account's row and enter your address. mySync opens your provider's page for making an app password, which you then paste in. An account already in Apple Mail can use Read My Email instead, with no password. mySync reads new mail from the moment you connect, not mail already there.

How does mySync read chat apps?

From their notifications. In Sources, open the app's page and tap Turn On Automatic Reading. The app must be installed with its notifications on. You can also share a message or take a screenshot of it.

How do I use Back Tap?

Set it up from its page in Sources. Then double-tap the back of your iPhone with a message on screen, and any plan in it waits in the Inbox.

How do I cancel, restore or get a refund?

In mySync, Settings has Manage Subscription and Restore Purchases. Apple handles billing, and you can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings › Delete All Data. It removes everything mySync has stored on your iPhone. Events and reminders you've added stay in your calendar and Reminders. The Privacy Policy has the details.
