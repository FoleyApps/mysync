Subscription

  • PlanFetch needs a subscription, monthly or annual, or Lifetime, bought once. In the US it's $2.99 a month, $19.99 a year or $49.99 for Lifetime, and the App Store shows the price where you are.
  • Lifetime doesn't renew and doesn't end. It doesn't cancel a subscription you already have: cancel that in Manage Subscription.
  • New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, your plan starts and your Apple Account is charged.
  • A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before it ends.
  • You can manage or cancel it in PlanFetch's Settings › Manage Subscription, or in your Apple Account's subscriptions. Canceling takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Apple handles billing and refunds. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • If a subscription ends, PlanFetch stops reading new messages. Plans waiting in the Inbox can still be confirmed, and events already in your calendar stay.

Check before you tap Add

PlanFetch reads messages with a small model and can get things wrong: a time, a place, or whether a plan is settled. That's why every plan waits for you. Check each one before you add it, and don't rely on PlanFetch alone for anything you can't afford to miss.
